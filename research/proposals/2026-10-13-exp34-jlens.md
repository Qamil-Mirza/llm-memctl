# §34 pre-registration: Jacobian-lens readout of the Qwen 2.5 3B and 7B readers on the §23 in-view questions

Date: 2026-10-13. Branch `jacobian-lens`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-jlens-readers-design.md`
(d094acb). Code at cc7529a. Written after the development folds (0, 1) were read and before any number from the
confirmation folds (2, 3, 4) was looked at. The pod computed all five folds in one run; the report tool
(`scripts/exp34_report.py`) refuses folds 2–4 without `--confirm`, and `--confirm` was not run before this file was
committed.

## 1. Question

§23 measured P(correct | all evidence in view under `fixed8`) at 0.397 for Qwen 2.5 3B against 0.667 for 7B. §34 asks
where, inside the 3B, the gold answer gets lost: is it never extracted from the evidence (outcome A), extracted mid-stack
and overridden late (B), or present to the final layer and still not produced (C)?

## 2. Instrument, fixed

- **Lens.** Anthropic's Jacobian lens (`external/jacobian-lens` at 581d398, `jlens` 0.1.0; the pod bundle carried no
  `.git`, so the sidecars record `jlens_commit: unknown`; the installed source is the pinned clone). One lens per
  reader, fitted on WikiText-103 test paragraphs (corpus sha256 `5f1bea06…246d91`), `max_seq_len` 128, `skip_first`
  16, `dim_batch` 32, bf16, seed 0, all source layers.
- **Prompt counts.** Target 128; the 7B's 90-minute box closed at **56 prompts** (5,419 s; `below_minimum: false`,
  minimum 32). Per the pod script's rule the 3B was then fitted on the **same 56 prompts** (same seed, same prefix;
  1,931 s). Both lenses average identical prompts; neither is refitted. Lens files: 7B sha256 `e3a07f9e…1329a2`,
  3B `ddbdb32d…de21b`. Every read and eval on disk carries its lens hash.
- **Readout.** Positions P1 (token before the gold's first content token, leading whitespace tokens teacher-forced after
  `Answer:`), P1_raw, P0, E, Q as in the spec. Tracked tokens: the gold's first content token, ` unknown`, `unknown`.
  Ranks are 1-based with strictly-greater ties. Final layer from the model's own logits for both lenses.
- **k = 10.** Outcome A: gold never in the top-10 at any layer; B: in the top-10 at some layer but not at the final
  layer; C: in the top-10 at the final layer. Emergence depth: first layer with rank ≤ 10, over n_layers.
- **Middle band** = layers [n//3, 2n//3): 3B (36 layers) [12, 24), 7B (28 layers) [9, 18).
- **Population.** Answerable questions with all evidence in view under `fixed8` that have an output and a §23 judge
  label for both readers, paired by `prompt_sha256` (the report stops on any mismatch). Development folds: 126 paired
  questions. Labels are the §23 single-judge verdicts (STATE.md caveat 3).
- **Statistics.** Paired bootstrap over questions, 10,000 resamples, 95% percentile intervals, seed fixed in the script.

## 3. Lens gate (spec §3), with the rulings made on the development folds

A reader's J-lens is used for its claims only if both parts pass:

- **(a)** At P1, log10(best rank of the gold over the middle band), logit lens minus J-lens, paired over questions:
  PASS if the interval's lower bound is above 0. *Ruling:* the spec's "mean log-rank over the band" is replaced by the
  best rank over the band, which is the quantity the outcomes use; recorded in the ledger before the run.
- **(b)** The lens-evaluation pass@10 (the jacobian-lens repository's six evaluation sets, readout over the band,
  trailing whitespace stripped, poetry read at the last newline), mean over sets: PASS if J-lens is **strictly** greater
  than the logit lens. A tie is a FAIL.
- If the gate fails for a reader, that reader's claims are reported twice: with the J-lens and with the logit lens
  (rows marked `logit (gate fallback)`). The J-lens rows stay in the report. Both appear in the result section.
- In confirmation mode the gate is computed on the development folds only and labelled so; the fallback follows it.

**Gate outcome on the development folds (fixed, carried into the confirmation report):**

| reader | (a) logit − J-lens, log10 best rank | (b) mean pass@10 J-lens / logit | gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3B | +1.219 (+0.907, +1.531) PASS | 0.014 / 0.039 FAIL | **FAIL → logit fallback rows** |
| 7B | +1.685 (+1.476, +1.896) PASS | 0.022 / 0.018 PASS | **PASS** |

The 3B fails (b) on the `typo` set (0.052 against 0.229); the other five sets are at or near zero for both lenses on
both readers with 56-prompt lenses. The 7B passes (b) by 0.004 on the mean. These are weak lenses; the fallback rule
exists for this case and is applied as written.

## 4. Claims (verbatim from spec §6; verdict rules fixed)

- **Claim 1 (size).** At P1, the 7B's best log-rank of the gold token is lower than the 3B's, paired by question:
  SHOWN if the interval of (3B − 7B) excludes 0 from above. Secondary: the 3B's share of outcome A exceeds the 7B's
  (paired difference of proportions, interval excluding 0).
- **Claim 2 (within the 3B).** Among in-view questions, the 3B's best log-rank at P1 is worse on the questions it gets
  wrong than on those it gets right: SHOWN if the AUC's lower bound is above 0.5. The 7B row is descriptive, no verdict.
- **The decomposition (no directional claim).** Among the 3B's in-view failures, the shares of A, B and C with bootstrap
  intervals, split by refusal (judge said `unknown`) versus wrong answer; the same for the 7B.
- **Descriptive.** Emergence depth; best rank at P0, E and Q; the ` unknown` trajectory on refusals; the community 7B
  lens cross-check (`andyx10`-derived file `community_qwen7b.pt`, sha256 `07a432fe…8531b3`, 100 prompts): top-10 Jaccard
  at P1 by layer third and Claim 1 recomputed with it; outcome shares by question type.

Claims are evaluated on the confirmation folds 2, 3, 4 (population to be reported) and also reported on all five folds.
Nothing in `scripts/exp34_report.py` or the readout code changes between this commit and the confirmation report.

## 5. Prediction, as recorded in the spec before any data, and its status on the development folds

Spec §7 predicted: the 3B's refusals are mostly B; the 7B shows C on the same questions; the 3B's wrong answers look
more like A; emergence depth is similar in both readers.

On the development folds the prediction is already contradicted: among the 3B's 32 refusals, B is 0.062 (0.000,
0.156), A is 0.375 and C is 0.562; among its 40 wrong answers B is 0.025, A 0.275, C 0.700. Emergence is not at a
similar depth: the 3B's gold emerges at a median fractional depth of 0.889, the 7B's at 0.464. This is reported here so
that the confirmation result cannot be read as a post-hoc story. The confirmation folds decide whether the development
picture holds; the prediction is not revised.
