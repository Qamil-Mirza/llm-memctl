## 1. Question

§23 measured P(correct | all evidence in view under `fixed8`) at 0.397 for Qwen 2.5 3B against 0.667 for 7B. §34 asks
where, inside the 3B, the gold answer gets lost: is it never extracted from the evidence (outcome A), extracted mid-stack
and overridden late (B), or present to the final layer and still not produced (C)?

## 2. Instrument, fixed

- **Lens.** Anthropic's Jacobian lens (`external/jacobian-lens` at 581d398, `jlens` 0.1.0; the pod bundle carried no
  `.git`, so the sidecars record `jlens_commit: unknown`; the installed source is the pinned clone). One lens per
  reader, fitted on WikiText-103 test paragraphs (corpus sha256 `5f1bea06…246d91`), `max_seq_len` 128, `skip_first`
  16, `dim_batch` 32, bf16, seed 0, all source layers.
- **Prompt counts.** Target 128; the 7B's 90-minute box closed at **56 prompts** (5,419 s; `below_minimum: false`,
  minimum 32). Per the pod script's rule the 3B was then fitted on the **same 56 prompts** (same seed, same prefix;
  1,931 s). Both lenses average identical prompts; neither is refitted. Lens files: 7B sha256 `e3a07f9e…1329a2`,
  3B `ddbdb32d…de21b`. Every read and eval on disk carries its lens hash.
- **Readout.** Positions P1 (token before the gold's first content token, leading whitespace tokens teacher-forced after
  `Answer:`), P1_raw, P0, E, Q as in the spec. Tracked tokens: the gold's first content token, ` unknown`, `unknown`.
  Ranks are 1-based with strictly-greater ties. Final layer from the model's own logits for both lenses.
- **k = 10.** Outcome A: gold never in the top-10 at any layer; B: in the top-10 at some layer but not at the final
  layer; C: in the top-10 at the final layer. Emergence depth: first layer with rank ≤ 10, over n_layers.
- **Middle band** = layers [n//3, 2n//3): 3B (36 layers) [12, 24), 7B (28 layers) [9, 18).
- **Population.** Answerable questions with all evidence in view under `fixed8` that have an output and a §23 judge
  label for both readers, paired by `prompt_sha256` (the report stops on any mismatch). Development folds: 126 paired
  questions. One read failed per reader on fold 2 (`_failed.json`); the confirmation population will be reported.
- **Labels.** The §23 single-judge verdicts (STATE.md caveat 3). The judge's errors are false negatives (about 0.1):
  some correct answers are counted as wrong or as refusals. This dilutes Claim 2 (right answers leak into the "wrong"
  group) and pushes the failure decomposition toward C (a sayable, produced answer labelled a failure).
- **Statistics.** Paired bootstrap over questions, 10,000 resamples, 95% percentile intervals, seed fixed in the script.

## 3. Lens gate (spec §3), with the rulings made before the pod run

A reader's J-lens is used for its claims only if both parts pass:

- **(a)** At P1, log10(best rank of the gold over the middle band), logit lens minus J-lens, paired over questions:
  PASS if the interval's lower bound is above 0. *Ruling (Task 6 review, before the run):* the spec's "mean log-rank
  over the band" is replaced by the best rank over the band, which is the quantity the outcomes use.
- **(b)** *Ruling against spec §3's wording ("ranks the expected token higher at mid layers"), in code since Tasks 5–6
  and made strict in commit 546aefe (2026-10-12 23:21 UTC), before the run:* the jacobian-lens repository's six
  evaluation sets, pass@10 with the readout over the band, trailing whitespace stripped (poetry read at the last
  newline), mean over the six sets: PASS if J-lens is **strictly** greater than the logit lens. A tie is a FAIL.
- If the gate fails for a reader, that reader's claims are reported twice: with the J-lens and with the logit lens
  (rows marked `logit (gate fallback)`). The J-lens rows stay in the report.
- In confirmation mode the gate is computed on the development folds only and labelled so (commit fb38b9b, before
  the run); the fallback follows it.

**Gate outcome on the development folds (fixed, carried into the confirmation report):**

| reader | (a) logit − J-lens, log10 best rank | (b) mean pass@10 J-lens / logit | gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3B | +1.219 (+0.907, +1.531) PASS | 0.014 / 0.039 FAIL | **FAIL → logit fallback rows carry the verdicts** |
| 7B | +1.685 (+1.476, +1.896) PASS | 0.022 / 0.018 PASS | **PASS, on (a); (b) is noise-sized** |

The 3B fails (b) on the `typo` set (0.052 against 0.229); the other five sets are at or near zero for both lenses on
both readers with 56-prompt lenses. The 7B passes (b) by 0.004 on the mean, about two items out of about 550: that is
below the 0.01 noise floor (STATE.md caveat 2) and does not by itself certify the 7B lens; gate (a), at +1.685 with a
lower bound of +1.476, is what carries the 7B gate. Because of this, every 7B J-lens row in the write-up is shown with
the 7B logit-lens row beside it (`logit (shown beside)`, produced by the report flag `--logit-rows-for qwen7b`, which
adds rows and changes no verdict, no gate and no statistic; commit 986ff36, before any `--confirm` run).

## 4. Claims (verbatim from spec §6) and which rows carry the verdicts

- **Claim 1 (size).** At P1, the 7B's best log-rank of the gold token is lower than the 3B's, paired by question:
  SHOWN if the interval of (3B − 7B) excludes 0 from above. Secondary: the 3B's share of outcome A exceeds the 7B's
  (paired difference of proportions, interval excluding 0).
- **Claim 2 (within the 3B).** Among in-view questions, the 3B's best log-rank at P1 is worse on the questions it gets
  wrong than on those it gets right: SHOWN if the AUC's lower bound is above 0.5. The 7B row is descriptive, no verdict.
- **The decomposition (no directional claim).** Among the 3B's in-view failures, the shares of A, B and C with bootstrap
  intervals, split by refusal (judge said `unknown`) versus wrong answer; the same for the 7B.
- **Descriptive.** Emergence depth; best rank at P0, E and Q; the ` unknown` trajectory on refusals; the community 7B
  lens cross-check; outcome shares by question type. The spec §6 descriptive "§33 evidence share against the §34
  outcome on the same questions" is dropped: §33 stopped at its sanity check and has no evidence shares.

**Verdict rows, fixed now.** The 3B's gate failed, so its J-lens is not a certified instrument. Therefore:
- Claim 1 is decided on the `logit (gate fallback)` row, which uses the logit lens for both readers (one instrument on
  both sides). The J-lens row is descriptive.
- Claim 2 for the 3B is decided on its `logit (gate fallback)` row. The J-lens row is descriptive.
- The decomposition for the 3B is reported on both rows; the logit row is the one quoted in the interpretation.
- The 7B's J-lens rows stand (gate passed on (a)), with the logit rows beside them for the reader to compare.

**Development-fold Claim 2, reported here because it sits at the noise edge.** 3B J-lens AUC 0.598 (0.499, 0.693),
NOT SHOWN; 3B logit AUC 0.598 (0.502, 0.692), SHOWN. The two lower bounds straddle 0.5 by 0.001 and 0.002. The
confirmation verdict follows the rule above and is reported with this caveat; a SHOWN at a lower bound within 0.01 of
0.5 is called marginal in the write-up, not a finding.

**Development-fold Claim 1, for the record.** 3B − 7B log10 best rank: J-lens +0.480 (+0.367, +0.601), logit +0.451
(+0.326, +0.581); share of A: +0.198 (+0.127, +0.270), logit +0.167 (+0.103, +0.230). All SHOWN.

## 5. Prediction, as recorded in the spec before any data, and its status on the development folds

Spec §7 predicted: the 3B's refusals are mostly B; the 7B shows C on the same questions; the 3B's wrong answers look
more like A; emergence depth is similar in both readers.

On the development folds the prediction is already contradicted, on both lenses: among the 3B's 32 refusals, B is
0.062 (0.000, 0.156) with the J-lens and 0.062 with the logit lens, A is 0.375 and C is 0.562 on both; among its 40
wrong answers B is 0.025 on both, A 0.275, C 0.700. Emergence depth (J-lens only; there is no logit emergence row, and
the 3B J-lens failed its gate, so this part rests on an uncertified instrument): the 3B's gold emerges at a median
fractional depth of 0.889, the 7B's at 0.464. This is reported here so that the confirmation result cannot be read as
a post-hoc story. The confirmation folds decide whether the development picture holds; the prediction is not revised.
