# §47 — the §44 bridge: content-specific interchange at the evidence span on natural LongMemEval rows (draft rev 0, Advisor, 2026-10-31)

Opened by the user (D-56, "go bridge skip") under §46 proposal §6: the fourth §46 verdict row opens the bridge on the
intervention arm alone. Thesis question (D-45): when the Qwen 3B fails with the evidence in view, is the evidence's
binding carried to and used by the answer computation (a failure downstream, of answer production) or not (a failure
of evidence use)? §44 asked this with first-token logit differences at the answer position and a swap manipulation that
did not reach validity (S0 0.816, lower bound 0.684). §46 built a tool that did reach validity on controlled cases:
interchange of the residual at the evidence token itself, with difference-matched and same-answer controls at 0 and
Transfer ≥ 0.70 through layer 15 of 36. §47 applies that tool, unchanged, to the §44 rows. **Not run; nothing here is a
result.** No probe (excluded until a case list varies names independently of base case, §46 results review §4), no
DAS / SAE / Patchscopes / new lens, no new rows, no steering. D-45 stopping rule: whichever outcome, documented.

## 1. Material (fixed; on disk, no model run to assemble)

- The 70 Qwen 3B rev 8 swap rows (`runs/exp44_swap/qwen3b_rev8/S/*.json`): clean prompt (gold G in exactly one
  evidence line), swap prompt (substitute S in its place, all other bytes identical), §23 label (success 38 / refusal
  14 / wrong 18), §44 class at 1 nat, forced-P1 logits, and the §44 P1 patching profile. Prompt lengths 808–2449
  tokens (median 1114).
- **Position alignment requires equal token count of G and S** (otherwise the suffix shifts and no residual position
  maps onto another). Under the Qwen tokenizer 36 of 70 rows qualify: **success 20, refusal 6, wrong 10** (counted by
  the Advisor from `n_tokens.clean == n_tokens.swap`; the Executor recounts from the token ids and reports the span
  length distribution, 1–6 tokens). The 34 unequal rows are excluded, stated as a limit (§7).
- **Following filter (as §44 rev 1 §B, 1 nat, already on disk):** a row enters the Transfer read only if its forced-P1
  preference flips with the evidence (F ≥ 1 nat, Δ_clean > 0 > Δ_swap). On all 70 rows this holds for success 31,
  refusal 11, wrong 15; the Executor reports the counts on the 36 aligned rows before any run. Rows that do not follow
  are run too (every pass is cheap) but are reported only in the descriptive outcome tables.
- **Honesty clause.** All 70 rows were read under §44 (logit Δ, classes, P1 patching) and §45 stage 3. There is no
  unread split; §47 is a **pooled read**, and every threshold below is copied from §46 verbatim, so no choice is made
  on these rows. The result is reported as "pre-registered thresholds, pooled rows previously read for other
  measurements".

## 2. Intervention (the §46 tool, moved to natural rows)

- **Site S1 = the gold span** in the evidence line (all its tokens; for a 1-token gold this is §46's P_B exactly).
  For each aligned row and each layer l = 0..35: the clean run with the span's residual (output of decoder layer l)
  overwritten by the swap run's at the same positions; generation from the clean prefix cache up to the span, suffix
  recomputed (the §46 position-indexed batched hook, 36 layers in one batch).
- **Readouts, both from the same pass:**
  - (i) **Logit transfer at forced P1** (the §44 measure): Δ = logit(G first token) − logit(S first token) at the first
    generated position under the §34 forced `Answer:` + whitespace; T(l) = (Δ_patched(l) − Δ_clean) / (Δ_swap −
    Δ_clean). Row-level "transferred at l" = T(l) ≥ 0.5. **Transfer(l)** = share of entered rows transferred at l.
  - (ii) **Generated outcome** (the §46 rev 4 lesson; greedy, max 64 new tokens, D1 normalisation as §44 U): G / S /
    unknown (the §38 refusal markers) / other. Used for the refuse-vs-answer reads (§4 B3) and for k3.
- **Controls, every entered row, every layer:**
  - **k1** difference-matched random vector of norm ‖h_swap − h_clean‖ at the same span and layer (seeded), §44 rev 6 /
    §46 k1: share with T ≥ 0.5 must be ≤ 0.10; its generated-outcome distribution is the baseline for B3.
  - **k2** same-answer donor is **not available** on natural rows (no third prompt with the gold unchanged and the
    span changed can be built without changing other bytes). Stated as a limit; k1 is the only perturbation control.
    (A mismatched donor from another row is not position-aligned and is not used.)
  - **k3** substitution floor: input embeddings of the span overwritten by S's; on equal-length rows this is the swap
    prompt itself, so k3 must reproduce the swap run's forced-P1 top-1 and generated outcome on ≥ 0.95 of entered rows.
    Below 0.95: reported as FAIL with the §46 direction-of-bias reading (a substitution that turns into an abstention
    lowers Transfer and cannot raise k1), and "capture path not proven exact" stated; bf16 batched vs batch-1
    numerics named as the first suspect. Fixed now, not after the read.
- **S2 = P1** (the §44 site), k1 only, layers 0, 3, …, 33, readout (i): a direct comparison with §44's forward patch on
  the same rows under the same hook; descriptive, no criterion.
- **Sanity, asserted per row:** T(0) under k3 ≈ 1 (|1 − T| < 0.05); T at the last layer ≈ 0 under the span patch (no
  layer after it can read the span; |T| < 0.05); exit on failure of either on > 5% of rows.

## 3. Criteria (copied from §46 §4; fixed now)

L*_class = the largest layer such that at every l ≤ L*: Transfer(l) ≥ 0.70, Wilson lower bound ≥ 0.55, k1(l) ≤ 0.10,
computed separately on **entered success rows** and **entered failure rows (refusal + wrong pooled)**. L* ≥ 10 counts
as mid-stack carriage. A class with fewer than 8 entered rows is reported descriptively (no L*); a class with 8–11
rows is read but labelled thin (the lower-bound bar then needs ≥ 7/8 … 10/11). Power stated in advance: with 16
failure rows the bars need ≥ 13/16 at every layer ≤ L*; with 20 success rows ≥ 16/20.

## 4. Reads and what each outcome means (fixed now)

| read | question | outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| **B0** k3 ≥ 0.95 | does the hook reproduce the swap prompt on natural rows? | pass: B1–B3 read as written; fail: read, with the direction-of-bias note and "not proven exact" |
| **B1** L*_success | does §46's mid-stack carriage hold on natural rows? | L* ≥ 10: yes, the §46 result is not specific to the toy task; L* < 10: the controlled-task result does not transfer, **B2 is read but reported as "no reference depth"**, the bridge closes |
| **B2** L*_failure vs L*_success | on in-view failures, does the evidence's binding still reach the answer computation at mid-stack? | L*_fail ≥ 10: the binding is carried and used on failures as on successes → the failure is downstream of evidence use (answer-production, layer-resolved, on these rows); L*_fail < 10 while L*_success ≥ 10: the binding is not carried on failures → evidence-use failure (layer-resolved); fewer than 8 entered failure rows: descriptive |
| **B3** refuse vs answer (descriptive unless ≥ 8 refusal rows enter) | does moving the binding change the decision? | on refusal rows: share whose generated outcome becomes G or S by layer, minus the same share under k1; on success rows: induced-abstention share by layer minus k1's (in §46 held the patch gave 14–17/55 abstentions at L17–29 against k1's 1–7/55) |
| **B4** descriptive | per-row L* medians by label (success / refusal / wrong) and by §44 class; S2 profile against §44's P1 forward patch | tables only |

No sentence about "suppressed", "erased" or "overridden". B2's reading is about the rows read (extractive single-hop,
equal-length substitutes) and is stated as such.

## 5. Build, order, cost

1. **Gate (free, no model run):** Executor recounts the 36 aligned rows from token ids, span lengths, following counts
   per label on the aligned rows, and names 2 success rows (longest prompt, shortest prompt) as the D-22 smoke. If
   fewer than 12 success or fewer than 8 failure rows enter, the user is told before any build; the bridge may then be
   read descriptively or closed ("the bridge cannot be read on these rows").
2. **Build** on the §46 code (`patch46.py` hook, readout; `exp44_swap.py` forced-P1 Δ and D1 marker code): a span
   site instead of a single position; both readouts from one pass; k1; k3; S2; report with the §3–§4 rules computed,
   never typed. Tests. Advisor build review. D-30 fresh-venv bundle test. Pre-launch stub check (full-size, peak RSS,
   per-pass time measured on the laptop stub).
3. **Pod:** one RunPod A40 secure. Work: 36 rows × 36 layers × (patch + k1) + k3 + S2 + baselines, prompts ~1100
   tokens with suffix recomputation, 64 new tokens. Advisor's estimate 15–30 min of GPU time from §46's rates (§46 did
   144 triples × 36 layers × 3 kinds in ≈ 10 min); **quote ≈ $0.30–0.50 at $0.59/h, hard stop $1.50**, programme after
   ≈ $22.2 of $30. The Executor's measured estimate from the stub must fit the quote. All D-54 conditions carry over:
   D-22 smoke on the pod with a projection against the stop, never two pods, the user types the approval in both
   panes, no pod left billing on a question, list-pods empty verified from both panes.
4. **Read once**, pooled (§1 honesty clause); the report is committed as it comes out; Advisor recounts from the raw
   per-row files before anything is relayed.

Laptop alternative: ≈ 2,600 generation passes at ~30–60 s each in fp32 on the CPU, 20–40 h; pod-only.

## 6. Prior art to cite in the write-up (not new)

2024 (binding under fine-tuning); Lavi et al. 2025 (unanswerability direction); Walters et al. 2026 ("represented, not
used"); Ling et al. 2026 (prompt-artefact share; §44 U1 = 0.228, "not the format"). §47 is an application of
interchange interventions (Geiger et al.) to natural failures; it claims no methods contribution.

## 7. Limits fixed now

Equal-length substitutes only (36 of 70 rows; biased toward short golds of the same surface type); extractive
single-hop rows (§44 rev 1 §F); 6 refusal rows make B3 descriptive by construction; no same-answer control; one model,
greedy, bf16 on the pod; forced-P1 first-token logits for readout (i); rows previously read for other measurements;
the §44 class labels are from a design whose validity read failed (they are used only to split tables, never as
inputs to B1–B3).

**Rulings.**

1. **Multi-occurrence rows and the span (rev 0 §1 corrected).** The number of differing tokens is 1: 18 rows, 2: 8,
   3: 4, 4: 3, 8: 2, 9: 1 (not "1–6"). The three rows with 8–9 differing tokens (c7cf7dfd, 8b9d4367, 3e321797) have the
   gold more than once in the evidence line and the swap replaces every occurrence (3e321797: three occurrences over a
   212-token range). **S1 = exactly the differing token positions, all occurrences, never the range between them.**
   Tokens between occurrences carry the swap's context and are not the binding; and only this definition keeps k3 exact
   (overwriting the input embeddings at the differing positions is the swap prompt byte for byte). The three rows are
   flagged `multi_occurrence`; B1 is read on all 13 entered successes (fixed now, before any run) and reported again
   without the three beside it, descriptive. Excluding them would drop success to 10 < 12 and fail the gate; that
   choice is made here, on the data's structure, not on any outcome.
2. **Thresholds (rev 0 §3 corrected).** At the 0.70 / 0.55 bars the Wilson lower bound needs 8/8 at n 8 (7/8 has LB
   0.529), 10/11 at n 11, **11/13 at n 13 (both entered classes)**, 13/16 at n 16, 16/20 at n 20 (Advisor verified).
   Power statement: each class needs ≥ 11/13 transferred at every layer ≤ L*; L* is read on 13 and 13.
3. **Two readouts from one patched suffix plus a fork (accepted).** The patched suffix (span → end of prompt, hook at
   the span positions only, 36 layer variants in one batch) is run once; its KV cache is copied; (i) the forced
   `Answer:` + whitespace tokens of §34 (`jlens_readout.readout_for`, byte-identical to §44) read Δ at P1 on one copy;
   (ii) greedy generation runs on the other. **Asserted sanity on the unpatched clean and swap rows:** |Δ − Δ_§44| <
   0.05 against the S files, exit on failure (> 5% of rows).
4. **Generated readout (rev 0 §2 (ii) corrected).** Stop at the end of the `Answer:` line, max 160 new tokens (the §45
