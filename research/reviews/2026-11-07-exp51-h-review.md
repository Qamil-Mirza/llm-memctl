## Branch M: submission 1 (two heads), dose 1.5; primary component L33 `o_proj`

| Test | Estimate | Holm (M family) | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 pathway | M1a revert-L33 0.293 [0.225, 0.368]; M1b vs random B6 0.717 | p 1e-4, **reject** | Pathway located |
| M2 causal path | M2a transplant 0.808; M2b clamp 0.193 | p 1e-4, **reject** | Causal path through the L33 output at P_ans |
| M3 account | predictor ΔAUROC −4.5e-5 (p 0.82); interaction 0.0005 [−0.025, 0.027] (p 0.49) | **not rejected** | Account **not supported** |

**Reading:**
- **Sufficient, not necessary.** Adding the edit's change to L33's output at the answer position alone, in the
  unedited model, releases 0.81 of FA rows. But removing it from the edited model (clamp) takes away only 0.19, and
  reverting L33 takes away 0.29. The L32 head appears to carry the rest, consistent with the S revert numbers
  (0.264 vs 0.078). Describe this as redundancy, observed, not proven.
- **The editor's account of what the component computes fails.** The f variable adds nothing to predicting false
  abstention: the baseline AUROC is already 0.998, a ceiling. Setting f in the unedited model has no different effect
  on answerable vs unanswerable questions than the matched logit bias does. So f behaves like a generic "stop saying
  unknown" push, not an answerability signal.
  - Two activity predictions held: evidence removed lowers f; city changed and unrelated-vs-absent are equivalent.
  - The M3 intersection-union fails on the predictor and interaction tests.
- **Addendum (2026-11-07, after re-reading submission 1).** The editor described the two heads as an
  evidence-independent "unknown" prior. A null interaction is close to what that account implies, so the frozen M3
  tests fit this account poorly. Read M3 as "not shown by the pre-registered tests", not as a refutation.
- **The M candidate is non-selective at its M dose** (S: Uns 0.96 under the total effect). Branch M locates where a
  release can be produced. It does not show a selective mechanism, and it says nothing about the R candidate's layer
  35 edit.
