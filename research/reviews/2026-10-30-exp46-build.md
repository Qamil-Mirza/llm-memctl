## 2. The readout (the one change required before the pod; proposal rev 4)

The Executor's laptop dev baselines (8 F1 triples, 21 rows read, $0, no held) show first-city agreeing with the §45
rule on 20/21, last-city also on 20/21, and the one disagreement is the case that matters: the model restates every
fact before concluding ("… Therefore, Kate's supervisor, Laura, works in Durham."). First-city reads the inserted
C1′ fact (wrong), the §45 rule says "other" because both cities appear (also wrong); only the conclusion is right.
The triple design *guarantees* C1′ is in every prompt, so this pattern is structural to §46, not rare. A readout that
scores the model's own correct conclusion as unsolved or as a transfer would bias both the solved filter and
Transfer(l) (a patched pass that restates facts first would read as C1′ at any layer).

**Ruling (rev 4):** the readout is the **conclusion**, fixed now, before any patched pass: F2 = the city on the
`Answer:` line (the §45 stop rule, max_new 160); F1 = the **last** pool city or "unknown" in the generation run to end
of turn or max_new **96** (48 can truncate a 14-fact restatement at H2). Classification by that last item:
C1 / C1′ / other / abstain; no city → other. `validate` no longer switches rules; it reports, on the dev baselines, the
three rules' agreement with each other and the solve rate under each, and lists disagreements. The same rule is used
for the solved filter, k3, Transfer and S2. `names_before_city` is computed on the text before the *conclusion* city.
Cost rises (patched generations run to the conclusion); the smoke projection and the $1.80 stop govern.
