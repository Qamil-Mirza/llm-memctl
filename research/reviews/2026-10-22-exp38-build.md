## 2. Rulings on the two open points

1. **Reproduction (⚠1): refusal flags only for R rows and pairs; the D1 verdict match is printed, not required.**
   The direction needs the answer-versus-refuse contrast, not B's exact answer; fp32 against the cache's bf16 vLLM may
   change a phrase without changing the contrast, and dropping such pairs would thin the 13 toward easy rows. For the
   8 damage rows, A must answer and pass D1 on the new engine (otherwise the damage baseline is not "already correct").
   The 0.8 bar over R plus damage (36) with R's own rate printed, and no backfilling of pairs, are accepted.
2. **Degenerate repeat (⚠2): a consecutive run of ≥ 8 identical token ids**, not ≥ 8 in total, so a list answer is
   not flagged. Empty and no-alphanumeric stand. Empty counts as degenerate in the classifier while the reproduction
   check keeps §23's convention; accepted, descriptive.

Points 3–10 of the Executor's list are accepted as stated (Control X = Random only, Reverse descriptive; lazy Random;
tie-break; zero-add at every sweep layer, self-overwrite at L35 and L7; automatic small-grid fallback; PROVISIONAL label;
stub bias; confirm-path test relabels dev folds and opens no confirmation file).
