## 2. What the outputs are (dev data, D1 string rule, PROVISIONAL; this is the design stage, so reading dev outputs is allowed)

At L25 α4 the direction removes "unknown" on 27 of 28 refusal rows, but most of what replaces it is not an answer in the
ordinary sense. Typical Steer outputs: "Total cost, user's expenses, total cost…", "Strugged, Strugged, Strugged, Strugged,…",
"Discounted price, discount, discount am…", "Unrelated data only, but goal achieveme…", "None mentioned, but Andy's attire
was n…". These are topic-word lists and hedges. The classifier calls them answers because the degenerate rule only catches
a run of ≥ 8 identical token ids; they are neither refusals nor the gold. D1 gold at this setting: Steer 3/28 = Steer-cut
3/28, both below B's own 6/28. On the 15 rows B refused (never used for estimation), Steer gold is 0/15.

At L29 α2 (ans 26/28) the outputs look like answers: "$55", "Serenity Yoga", "Tom", "Red Light District Hostel", "1998
Views", "Blue Snaggletooth action figure", "The Plesiosaur had a blue scaly body…". D1 gold 8/28 (B: 6/28); on the 15
unseen rows 2/15, the only setting with gold on rows the estimation never saw. Steer-cut was not run there (controls run
at the chosen setting only, per §5.4).

Two further facts from the rows. Steer and Steer-cut both answer on 21 rows and give the same phrase on none of them,
so the steered text is prompt-conditioned, not a fixed string. Only 2 of 27 Steer phrases appear verbatim in the intact
prompt: the direction makes the model generate about the topic, not copy from the evidence.

Reading: on dev at the pre-registered setting this is §4's third case, "the direction makes the model answer, but what it
says is not the gold", with the sharper note that "answer" here is largely word salad the pre-registered classes do not
separate. The selection rule (highest ans) rewards exactly that salad, which is why the ans-maximising setting is not the
gold-maximising one. This is a weakness of the design gate I wrote, visible only now; it is recorded, not patched.
