## Recount finding: DE's margin is partly read at the wrong token (post hoc; the verdict is unchanged)

**What I found.** In DE, **49 of 94 answered rows begin their answer with a name, not the city**, for example "Answer: David lives in York". In TU it is 15 of 258; in RC, 0 of 223.

**Why it matters.**
- For those rows the token after `Answer:` is the name. So M_city and M_commit at P_ans are not read at the step where the model commits to the city.
- This explains why DE's *answered* rows have a mean M_commit of −3.05: the next token is neither the city nor "unknown".

**Sensitivity check (post hoc, exploratory, held; it does not revise the pre-registered state).** With answered rows restricted to answers that begin with the city (45 of them):
- fine cells: DE Δ_M −4.36 [−5.96, −3.12], covering 76 false abstentions;
- coarse cells: −5.03 [−6.29, −3.90].

The direction is unchanged and larger. The pre-registered DE number (−1.209) stands in the record, with this caveat attached.

**Lesson for any follow-up.** Read the margin at the position that produces the first city in the model's own output, as in the F1 fix. Do not read it at P_ans when the answer may restate the subject.

## What this shows (wording for EXPERIMENTS.md and the slides)

1. **The elicited sufficiency measure failed.** Asked "does the memory give enough information…?", Qwen2.5-3B says "no" on most answered rows in RC and TU, on every unanswerable row, and to the reversed question as well. It is a "no"-leaning response, not a sufficiency judgement. So §50 cannot say whether false abstentions involve a sufficiency misjudgement.
2. **The city margin is lower on false abstentions** than on answered rows of the same task, hardness and city, in DE and RC. It is also lower within the 47 content-matched flip pairs.
   - It is still large (about 10–16 logits): the model clearly prefers the right city over the other cities even when it refuses.
   - What it loses is the comparison against "unknown". Only about a quarter to a third of false abstentions are near ties (|gold − unknown| < 1 logit).
3. **This is the pattern the "not sure enough" account predicts.** It cannot confirm that account, for three reasons:
   - M_city is an output-side margin, not a validated confidence measure.
   - The alternative could not be measured.
   - DE's margin is partly read at the wrong token (see above).
4. **No causal claim.** Stage 2 did not open.
