## 3. What I ran beyond the reports (folds 2-4, 3B, via memctl.jacobian_data.load_records + the read files)
- Every read has P1 = P0 + 2: `Answer:` is appended straight after the generation prompt (spec §4). Yet 200 of the
  204 cached 3B outputs put reasoning text first and `Answer:` later (74 successes, 65 refusals, 61 wrong). So P1 is
  a forced immediate answer, not the position where the cached answer, or the "unknown", was produced.
- 3B successes with logit outcome A: 17 (= 0.221 x 77). 11 have a final answer that does not start with the gold's
  first token text (e.g. gold "four" / answer "4"). 6 do start with it, case-insensitively (e.g. "IKEA" for token
  "IKE", "doc martin" for "Doc"), and are still A. So paraphrase and format explain part of the success-A share,
  not all of it. Answers reached only through the reasoning passage are a second explanation the data allow.

## 4. Overreach (a descriptive table carries no causal claim)
- "C is the 3B's failure mode." C is 0.638 of failures and 0.727 of successes. What separates failures from
  successes is A (0.346 against 0.221) and the Claim 2 rank gap, not C.
- "The decision to say 'unknown' is made at the very end, with the answer in hand" and "the answer is there at the
  output and is not produced". These describe depth inside one forward pass at a forced position the model never
  answered from. "In hand" (top-10) is not "extracted". And "the gold sits a few ranks below it" has no row behind
  it: no report gives the gold's final-layer rank on refusals, and the median of 3 is over all 204 questions and is
  the best rank over layers.
- "A has a paraphrase floor ... net of that floor about 0.1." The subtraction is post hoc and assumes success-A is
  pure measurement artefact, which §3 above contradicts in part. Judge false negatives move success-profile items
  into failures, so the true failure A share is if anything above 0.346. That works against the netting.
- "B does not happen ... Nothing is found and then lost." On all folds the deciding row gives B 0.025 (0.005, 0.050)
  for failures. On the confirmation folds the 3B's successes have B 0.052 (0.013, 0.104). B is rare and no more
  common in failures than in successes. It is not absent.
- Emergence: "finds it in the last tenth ... in the same layers where ` unknown` is winning". The median is 0.889
  with q1 0.590, so a quarter of emergences come before 0.6. The 0.90 ` unknown` depth is over 65 refusals, but
  emergence is over 138 questions of all outcomes: two populations. Both rows come from the failed-gate 3B J-lens.
- "What this changes": "the loss is output-side", "the 3B does extract the answer", "a late refusal/decision
  mechanism", "points away from more or better-placed evidence". These are mechanism and policy claims. No
  intervention was run (stage 2 not opened), and no evidence was varied. Claims 1-2 (pre-registered, SHOWN) say the
  gold is less available at P1 in the 3B and in its failures, which is not purely an output-side picture.
- "Both lenses do what a J-lens should": the 3B lens failed gate (b). Only gate (a) supports this sentence.

## 5. Judge caveat and noise floor
- The direction is stated. But "false negatives would show up as C anyway" is inexact: they show the success
  profile (logit C 0.727, A 0.221, B 0.052). "About 0.1" needs its base: about 20 of 204 answers, i.e. about 0.16 of
  the 127 failures.
- The noise floor is handled for 7B gate (b) and Claim 2. The B shares (0.000-0.031) are floor-sized point
  estimates, so the text should give their intervals rather than "does not happen".
