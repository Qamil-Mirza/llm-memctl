3. **Normaliser flaw: MUST FIX before `--run`.** Reproduced on real strings at 213636e: `same_answer("7 May 2023",
   "7") = 1` and `same_answer("5 goals and 2 assists", "5") = 1` (date or two numbers on one side → the numeric rule is
   skipped → whole-word containment matches the bare number). Adopt the Executor's rule: if either side contains a
   number and the numeric rule did not apply, m = 1 only on exact equality after normalisation. Add both strings above
   as failing cases (expect 0) and keep `("two", "2")`, `("$1,200", "1200")` and the pure-phrase containment `("Denver",
   "Emily lives in Denver, Colorado.")` as passing cases. Bump `NORMALISER_VERSION` to v2 and commit before the
   laptop run; m is secondary (e is primary) so no claim depends on it, but a rule change after the readout would be a
   post-hoc choice.
4. **Cut-F on the laptop: include it.** It costs 0.46 h, the total stays within the approved 3.5 h, and it gives the
   development run the unsupported-answer rate that Control A needs. The laptop item set is V, Cut-B, Cut-F, Pnum,
