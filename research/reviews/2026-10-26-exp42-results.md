## Reading

1. **The policy fails on its own terms.** With a penalty of one per wrong answer, the selective policy scores −6 of
   120, the same as steering every refusal, five below the §35 instruction line, and six below abstaining. The 20
   rows it chose came out right 7 times and wrong 13. A policy at λ 1 needs more than half of its fires to be right;
   the top 30% by s_proj reach 0.35, and no frozen threshold between 10% and 90% is positive (best −1 at q 0.7).
   The post hoc curve is negative at every q too, so this is not a matter of where the threshold sits.
2. **The signal replicates as a ranking and still cannot carry a policy.** AUROC 0.765 (0.677, 0.843) against 0.803 in
   §40. A ranking this good over a base rate of 0.275 does not lift the top of the list past 0.5. Steering a refusal
   on LoCoMo is right about one time in four, and s_proj enriches that to about one in three, not one in two.
3. **s_proj does not know whether the question is answerable.** On the 36 unanswerable rows, the policy fires on 5
   of the 20 step-0 refusals (0.25), against 20 of 127 (0.157) on the answerable rows; AUROC of s_proj for
   answerable-refusal against unanswerable-refusal is 0.443. So the signal tracks "a push here would produce the
   stored answer" and not "there is an answer in the evidence". That is consistent with §34c (the 3B's unknown
   signal is insensitive to evidence) and is the reason Control U moves the wrong way (0.444 → 0.583).
4. **§40's effect replicates at a smaller size.** +0.233 (0.150, 0.317) against +0.333 in §40; Steer-all gold 0.275
   against 0.358, below rev 1's 0.30 "meaningful" line. Control X passes. Steer beats the §35 line by +0.133.
   Temporal questions get nothing from steering (0.062 of 32); multi-hop gets the most (0.429 of 21).
5. **The damage check sits on the bar, and pooled it crosses it.** Control C is −0.225 (−0.350, −0.100), with the
   upper bound exactly at −0.100; rev 1's rule is "harmful if UB < −0.10", so by the letter it is not harmful, and the
   record should say it is a tie on the bar. Pooling §40's and §42's 80 correct rows (descriptive, not pre-registered)
   gives −0.2125 (−0.300, −0.125): across both fresh sets the every-token push costs about a fifth of the answers the
   3B already had right. This belongs in the §42 text and in STATE F6.
6. **§40's one-position qualification does not replicate.** Steer-all − Steer-0 on gold is +0.017 (−0.042, 0.075)
   here against +0.075 (+0.017, +0.142) in §40, first token identical 120/120 again. Descriptive; the §40 text should
   keep its figure and note that the fresh rows did not reproduce it.
