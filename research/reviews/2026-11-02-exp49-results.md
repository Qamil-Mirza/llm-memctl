## What this shows (wording for EXPERIMENTS.md and the slides)

1. **The primary test passes, in a narrow sense.** Pushing the internal state toward the decoded city turns false "unknown"s into the right city far more often than a random push of the same size: +0.836 [+0.774, +0.894].
   - R1's output city equals the probe's pick ĉ on every trial: ANS 165/165, UNA 144/144, CF 51/51. So its gold rate is the P_ans decode accuracy, and the repair adds nothing beyond the decode.
2. **It is not selective.** The same push turns every genuine "unknown" into a made-up city (144/144). It is an answer switch, not a repair that respects missing evidence.
3. **The decoded target adds nothing over a content-free push.**
   - R2, the §38 one-position refusal-direction push, recovers as much: R1 − R2 = −0.024 [−0.069, +0.013]. That is no difference shown, not equivalence.
   - R2 costs far less on genuine abstentions: 0.188 against 1.000.
   - Removing the "unknown" option (R4) recovers everything, with unsupported 0.451 and half of genuine cases degenerate.
4. **These false abstentions are mostly not hidden answers.**
   - In 152 of 165, the model's own note already names the gold city before it writes "Answer: unknown".
   - The probe cannot decode the answer before the note: P_pre is below the candidate floor on every subset.
   - So the information is in the visible output, not "legible inside" in the *Legible Failures* sense.
   - Plain description: **the model writes the answer down, then refuses to commit to it.**
   - This is a finding about the population. It is consistent with §35's note-plus-instruction result. It is not a mechanism.
5. **The comparison with wrong-answer repair cannot be read.**
   - The *Legible Failures* repair did not reproduce on our F1 wrong answers (+0.043, CI includes 0).
   - So Q-6 is "not interpretable" as pre-registered (B6).
   - We cannot say that repair works for abstentions but not for wrong answers, or the reverse.
6. **Gates (secondary).**
   - The sufficiency gate separates the two kinds of "unknown" almost perfectly on these templates (AUROC 0.999; fires 96.4% vs 0.7%). U is +0.235 over never-repair.
   - Per Codex's point 1 this is **template-limited**. Do not call it evidence-sufficiency recognition.
   - The UNA cases differ from ANS by fixed templates (replaced relations and distractors).
7. **Q-3 tracking (H1, 51 bases):** R1 and R2 each give the counterfactual city 50/51 times. The released answer follows the evidence when the evidence changes. The two arms are not compared.
