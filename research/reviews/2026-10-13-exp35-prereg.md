**Verdict: REVISE.** No held-out leak, and every development number in the draft reproduces. But four things are
not decidable as written: Claim 1's conditioning set is too small for its 0.5 bound, the deciding derivation
criterion D2 is a permissive prompt shared with the outcome judge, arm F replaces one `unknown` clause when the
cached prompt carries two, and the frozen-note arms have no stated mechanics for teacher-forcing through the chat
endpoint. Claim 3 also compares a regenerated arm with cached accuracy across hosts and a different question set.
All fixes are text; none needs a fold 2–4 number.

## 2. Decidability and power
- Claim 2 and the cost numbers are single pre-stated tests with a direction and a bound. Claim 3 is descriptive by
  design and says so. Claim 1 is a single test but its bound is arbitrary and the set is too small to carry it.
- Size: §34b gives 62 marked refusals on folds 2–4; at the dev D1 rate (0.23) that is about 14; D2 may differ. I
  computed the smallest share whose 10,000-resample lower bound clears 0.5: n 12 → 10/12 (0.83); n 14 → 11/14
  (0.79); n 15 → 12/15 (0.80); n 22 (all folds) → 16/22 (0.73). The test can only pass if about four in five
  derived-then-refused questions turn correct. The draft's own prediction ("most") is not that strong.
- The 0.5 bound is justified only by the word "majority". The thing the arm isolates is "changing only the answering
  instruction turns a refusal into the right answer, more than teacher-forcing noise does": that is a paired contrast
  against arm A on the same questions, not a raw share against 0.5.
- **Must change:** (a) decide Claim 1 as the paired difference P(B correct) − P(A correct) on the conditioning set,
  SHOWN if the lower bound is above 0; report the raw share s and "s above 0.5" as descriptive. (b) State in advance:
  if the conditioning set on folds 2–4 is below 15 the verdict is UNDERPOWERED (not NOT SHOWN), the all-folds set
  (about 22) is reported as the pooled estimate, labelled as mixing development and confirmation folds, and no
  "selection gap exists" sentence enters STATE.md on it. (c) Pre-state the two rejections: Claim 1 passes and Claim 2
  fails means a threshold shift; Claim 1 fails and arm A reproduces the refusal on under 0.8 of the set means
  teacher-forcing failed, not selection.

## 3. Criterion D2: too permissive, and the same judge as the outcome
- `judge.py` `_LME_STEPS` answers yes when the response "contains all the intermediate steps to get the correct
  answer". A note that lists the two appointments without saying "2" is D2-true. For a counting or comparison question
  that is a note with the facts and no derived answer, so D2 includes exactly the cases the arm is meant to exclude
  (derivation still to do at answer time). The per-type prompts make it worse: `single-session-preference` is a rubric
  ("recalls and utilizes the user's personal information"), `knowledge-update` accepts "previous information along
  with an updated answer". D2's meaning varies by type; D1 does not.
- Claim 2 is a contrast between D2-true and D2-false refusals on B's judged correctness. Both verdicts come from the
  same Qwen-7B judge against the same gold; its false negatives (STATE caveat 3, about 0.1, paraphrase-driven) hit
  the note and the answer of the same question together, which makes D2-true and B-correct co-vary through the judge,
  not the model. That inflates Claim 2.
- **Must change:** D1 decides Claims 1 and 2 (string, deterministic, already fixed on dev; its over-count of copied
  evidence is conservative for Claim 1 and attenuates Claim 2). D2 is reported beside, with a dedicated prompt fixed in
  the proposal ("Does the note state the answer to the question? yes or no", not the LongMemEval steps prompt), and
  the two criteria's agreement is reported. If the Advisor keeps D2 deciding, it must use a judge other than the
  arm-B judge and say so.

## 4. Arm B placement, leak, and the teacher-forcing mechanics
- Leak: the inserted line contains no gold, no candidate, no type. OK. It asserts "The note above names the facts"
  on D-false notes too; fixed string, acceptable, but it is a mild nudge to answer from the note; say so.
- Mechanics are unstated and are not trivial. The §23 prompt is one **user** message to `/chat/completions`
  (`memctl/llm.py` 144–147: temperature 0, seed 0); the note is the assistant turn. "Prompt + cached note + line +
  `Answer:`" therefore either (1) continues the assistant turn (`add_generation_prompt=False`,
  `continue_final_message=True`, or the raw `/v1/completions` route on `wrap_chat`'s rendered text, as
  `jlens_readout.py` does), in which case the instruction sits inside the model's own message as text it "wrote"; or
  (2) is a second user turn after the note, which is a dialogue, not a frozen note. These are different experiments.
- Arm A's reproduction check is necessary but has no bound (line 47: "expected ... on most questions"). §33 gives the
  prior: 16 of 20 final answers reproduce under transformers; under the same vLLM build it should be higher.
- **Must change:** fix route (1), name the endpoint and flags, and require an identity check: the rendered token
  ids of (chat text + cached note up to `Answer:`) must equal the §34b own-position `n_tokens` minus `n_leading_ws`
  for every question (the read files store `prompt_sha256`, `n_tokens`, `n_leading_ws`, `answer_marker`). Pre-state
  the arm A bound: cached answer reproduced (same judge verdict class) on at least 0.8 of the conditioning set, else
  Claims 1–2 are reported as "teacher-forcing not reproduced" with no verdict.

## 5. Arm F: two `unknown` clauses, and the replacement text
- The cached prompt (I printed a fold-0 record) begins with the environment goal "Answer with a short phrase that
  states the fact asked for. **If the memory does not contain the answer, reply: unknown**" and then
  `REASONING_INSTRUCTIONS` with "**Reply unknown only if** the memory has nothing at all on the topic." Two clauses;
  `DEFAULT_INSTRUCTIONS` (draft line 11) is not the text used, `task.goal` is (`llm.py` build_prompt). The draft
  replaces "the instruction's unknown clause" (singular). Replacing one leaves a prompt that both forbids and permits
  `unknown`.
- The replacement "Never reply unknown; give the single best-supported short answer" also restates the answer format
  and adds "best-supported", a confidence frame absent from the original. Small, but it is more than the abstention
  rule, and arm B's line carries the same phrase, so B and F share a wording change the draft attributes to abstention.
- **Must change:** quote both original clauses and both replacements verbatim; delete the first clause and replace
  the second with "Never reply unknown." only; drop "best-supported" from B's line too (or keep it in both and name it
  as part of the intervention). Record the exact §35 prompt strings in the proposal, not in code.

- Missing: arm F on the 30 abstention questions costs 30 of 30 false answers by construction. The draft calls F "the

- Per-type split: descriptive only, no verdict; preference and knowledge-update rows flagged as least trustworthy
  (STATE caveat 3). The 7B under A/B/F: descriptive, as drafted, but (i) and (ii) should be printed for it too.

## 7. Noise floor
- ±0.007 is batch-level accuracy noise on about 470 questions; Claim 3's "under 0.01 is noise" is right for the 165
  marked questions only roughly (one flipped answer is 0.006). Claim 1 on about 15 questions is where regeneration
  variance bites: 16 of 20 final answers reproduced in §33 means about three of 15 could move on their own. That is
  why Claim 1 needs arm A as its paired control (finding 2), not a raw share. With that change Claim 3 is
  interpretable as drafted; without it, no.

## 8. vLLM against transformers: ruling
- §23 answers came from vLLM (`backend: openai`, pod server). §33, §34, §34b, §34c ran transformers 5.17.0 bf16
  (`exp34_pod.sh` 21, 52; `jlens_read.py` 194) and §33 showed transformers greedy reproduces only 10 of 20 cached
  outputs exactly. The draft specifies vLLM (lines 42, 99). **Ruling: generating with transformers would be a design
  change and must be stated in the proposal before the run, with arm N added (finding 6) so every comparison is
  within-backend; arm A's reproduction rate is then expected lower and its 0.8 bound must be re-justified in text.
  Under vLLM, teacher-forcing needs the route of finding 4.** Either way the backend, build and sampling flags go in
  the proposal and the report header.
