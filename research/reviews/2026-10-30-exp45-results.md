## 2. The echo artefact (confirmed, and wider than reported)

The Executor's finding: for the J-lenses the first-readability depth is 0.00 on every task and the stage-2 departure
layer is 0 on most rows, because at layer 0 a J-lens decodes the token being fed in, and F1 outputs restate the facts,
so the gold is itself an input token at a later generated position. Advisor check on the 228 solved F1 prompts whose
output contains the gold: at the 233 generated positions whose input token *is* the gold, the gold's layer-0 rank is
≤ 7 on 233/233 for j56 and for jrefit (rank 1 on 54 and 41), and ≥ 8 on 233/233 for the logit lens. Confirmed.

It is wider than layer 0. The logit lens shows no depth-0 artefact, but its 1.00 readability on the fixed criterion
also rests on positions after the gold has been produced: at such positions the residual still carries the token
identity at layers 30–33. Two readings remove the echo in two strengths:

- **Rule A (input-token exclusion):** a hit on target X at counted position p is not counted when X is the token being
  fed in at p. Everything else as the fixed rule. This removes exactly the mechanism identified.
- **Rule B (production positions):** count only counted positions up to and including the one that produces the first
  occurrence of X in the output; once X has been generated, later positions do not count for X. This is the reading
  "readable before the answer is produced". In F2 the note usually states the gold before `Answer:`, so under rule B most
  F2 rows have no production position inside the counted span and read as not readable *by construction*: for F2, rule
  B measures whether the note already held the answer, not readability. Rule B is therefore read per format.

## 3. Ruling (post hoc, labelled; no new run; $0)

The pre-registered criterion counted positions where the lens can only be echoing the token just produced, so its
readability and depth columns do not measure what §45 asked. The rules below change the readout after the data exist,
so everything they produce is **post hoc** and is reported with that word next to it; the pre-registered columns stay
in the report, marked "inflated by echo". The held half still decides and dev is shown first.

1. **Fixed criterion:** kept in every table as pre-registered; its depth quartiles and its "recovers" verdicts carry the
   note "inflated by echo (§45 results review); not evidence of readability before production". The stage-2 departure
   layers under the fixed rule are struck as an artefact (172 of 287 at layer 0 for j56).
2. **Rule A** (input-token exclusion, defined above) is the reading for "readable anywhere in the answer span"; **rule B**
   (production positions) is the reading for "readable before the answer is produced". Both are printed for every
   stage-1 row, by task and by format (F1, F2) as well as pooled, with Wilson intervals and the 0.70 / 0.55 bar applied
   descriptively. Depth quartiles are printed under A and B only.
3. **Stage 2:** departure layers and the "not readable before the final layer" share under rules A and B, by half.
4. **Stage 3:** readability of the gold (clean) and the substitute (swap) under rule B, by §23 label and §44 class.
5. **Lens sentence:** "the 56-prompt lens is stable (agreement 0.998, Spearman 0.980); its 128-prompt refit scores the §34
   gate identically (0.014 vs the logit lens's 0.039) and reads the maps the same way; neither J-lens adds anything over
   the logit lens on the 3B".
6. **No §46.** The maps name no intermediate or transition readable before layer 30 of 36 for any lens, task or role, so
   there is no causal target; the D-45 stopping rule applies: document, do not invent.

## 4. What §45 shows (the plain reading for EXPERIMENTS.md §45, draft)

- On the solved controlled prompts the answer token becomes readable in the top-10 only in the last six layers (first
  readable layer 30–33 of 36, depth 0.83–0.92), at the position that produces it (F1: 1.00 of 49 / 37 / 25 rows) or at
  the answer position after a note that already states it (F2, rule A 1.00; by rule B the note already held it on
  0.74–1.00 of F2 rows). No lens reads it earlier at any counted position, and at the last prompt token it is readable
  on 0.00–0.14 of rows.
- The relational intermediate (RC: the supervisor's name) and the temporal competitor (TU: the old city) are readable
  only in the same late band, and the competitor is not readable at the answer position in F2 at all (0.00 by rule B):
  the model's answer position carries the new city and nothing of the old one that a lens can see before the final layer.
- Matched failures: the gold is readable before the final layer at the production positions on about a third of them
  (rule B 0.33 held) and anywhere in the answer span on about two thirds (rule A 0.65), the latter because failing
  outputs restate the facts; where a failure departs from its matched success, it does so at layer ≥ 30 (189 of 287
  pairs for j56 under rule A; 95 pairs never separate). The lens cannot see a failure coming before the last six layers.
- On the §44 rows the gold is readable before the final layer at the model's own answer position on about half of the
  clean rows (0.44–0.50) and on 0.29 of the refusals; the substitute on about a third of the swap rows. Median first
  layer 32.
- Nothing here is about the model's computation: "not readable" means "not recovered by these lenses" (proposal §5).
  The lenses agree with each other and with the logit lens, and a 128-prompt refit changes nothing, so the three
  readouts are one readout. §45 adds to §34: the 3B's answer appears late in the stack in every format and task tried,
  and no earlier intermediate is readable with these tools.

## 7. Addendum (Executor's recount 34c0472 / c70cb71): four corrections to §2, all accepted

   clean rows" in §4 holds under rule A only. The §45 text says so.
2. **DE under rule B: 0.62/0.62/0.60 (held, all formats), F2 0.22/0.22/0.17**, not 0.64/0.64/0.62 and 0.26/0.26/0.22. The
