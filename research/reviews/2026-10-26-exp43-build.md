## Llama stage-1 script (6342460)

Read: populations (G = §35 population, marked, cached refusal; P0 = marked, answerable, not all in view, cached
refusal; `_abs` rows excluded; disjointness asserted), the gate arithmetic (`setting_gate`: answers ≥ ANS_MIN,
degenerate ≤ 1, gold(Steer) − gold(cut) ≥ 2, Random ≤ 0.2 × Steer answers), the rule order (deeper layer, then
smaller α), the alternative order printed as descriptive, exits by stage, folds other than 0–1 refused.

Rulings on the builder's seven points:
1. Tie-break: as rev 1; the §38-style "most answers" choice beside it is fine as descriptive.
2. **Change:** the gate's answer bar must scale with the rows that reproduce. Use `ANS_MIN = ceil(0.5 × n_G_ok)`
   (7 when all 13 reproduce), not an absolute 7; the gold bar stays ≥ 2 rows; the reproduction bar 0.8 over G + P0
   stands. A dropped G row is listed in the gate json with the reason.
3. **Change:** minimum pairs 5, not 2 (exit 7 below that; Part A for Llama then closes as "too few pairs", a
   result). Two pairs would make v_L a near-random direction and a gate pass meaningless.
4. Cut at every setting that meets the answer and degenerate criteria; Random in rule order until one passes: fine.
5. N check 5 rows ≥ 4/5 on first id and marker, seed [38, L]: fine.
6. Phi's lens fit with BOS off, matching its chat prompts: fine, and recorded in the config.
7. Partner configs belong to the Part B pod build: fine.

Power concern (only 2 of the 13 gate rows carry the gold in the cached note): the gold bar stays at ≥ 2. The note is
the model's own text before `Answer:`; the evidence is still in the prompt, so the gold can be read from it, as it
was on Qwen. The report prints "gold in cached note" per G row as a descriptive column, so a FAIL can be read against
it. The bar is not lowered after seeing the count.

The lens dev reads (Llama, Gemma, Phi; folds 0–1; logit lens only) may run on ccaff40 now: B1 is verified bit-identical
for Qwen, the reads are free and descriptive, and they are rerun if anything in this review had changed the encode.
