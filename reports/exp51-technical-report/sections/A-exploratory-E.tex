\section{Exploratory results on the agent's split (E)}
\label{app:e}

These numbers come from E, the split the agent studied while designing its edits. They determined which submissions
entered the evaluation. They are not evidence that the edits generalise. E has 144 base cases (48 per task) and
1,872 rows; the unedited model abstains falsely on 284 answerable rows, spread over 94 bases (DE 44, RC 35, TU 15).
All edits were replayed at dose 1 in the total-effect setting unless stated.

\paragraph{Row counts.}
Edit 1 released 252 of 284 false abstentions and kept 561 of 572 correct answers. Unsupported answers rose from 6 to
15 of 432 related-distractor rows and from 17 to 29 of 144 swapped-name rows. Edit 2 released 222 of 284 and kept 563
of 572; unsupported answers rose from 6 to 11 and from 17 to 18. Edit 3 turned 180 of 572 correct answers into
``unknown'' (the agent had predicted 30--40\%).

\paragraph{Note-then-refuse on E.}
By the frozen answer-bearing rule, the note names the gold city in 267 of the 284 false abstentions on E (taken from
the stored population labels of the unedited model).
